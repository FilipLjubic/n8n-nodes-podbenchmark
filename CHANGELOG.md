- Simplified Compare Suppliers items lead with supplier, delivered total and currency, so they read well in n8n's table view.

- First release: Product (Search, Get, Get Options, Compare Suppliers, Find by Supplier Link), Category (Get Many) and Supplier (Get Many, Get, Get Price Standing, Match to Shop) operations against the free POD Benchmark API.
